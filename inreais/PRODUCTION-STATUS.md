# INREAIS DELIVERY — Production status

Updated: 2026-11-22

## Editorial scope
- Food-delivery couriers only: bicycle, motorcycle and car.
- Platforms: Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat, Glovo, iFood, Rappi, DoorDash and local food-delivery apps.
- Regions: Brazil, Portugal, United Kingdom and Ireland.
- Excludes parcel delivery and warehouse logistics unless directly relevant to food-delivery couriers.
- Output: TikTok and YouTube Shorts, vertical 9:16, maximum 60 seconds; target 45–55 seconds in Brazilian Portuguese.
- The owner is the final editor. No automatic publishing.

- Editorial configuration: `inreais/editorial-config.json` (food-delivery niche).

- Dashboard: `inreais/index.html`.

## Production safeguards
1. Open the original source and verify publication date, country, people and platform.
2. Corroborate material claims independently; label allegations and unknowns.
3. Seek creator confirmation for personal stories, eyewitness footage and creator-originated claims where appropriate.
4. Never reuse third-party clips without permission, a licence or a documented legal basis.
5. Use original or appropriately licensed visuals; label AI reconstructions as illustrative.
6. Obtain owner approval for the story, script, visuals, final edit, title and caption before publication.
