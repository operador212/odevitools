Updated: 2026-11-22

3. Identify active women food-delivery creators in Portugal.
4. Recheck current follower counts, recent posting frequency and platform links for every creator before using metrics in editorial decisions.
5. Track creators who transparently show gross earnings, hours online, distance, fuel/charging, vehicle costs and expenses. Never infer net income from a single screenshot or headline.

## Editorial and privacy rules

- Monitor only public posts and publicly available profile information.
- Do not download/repost creator footage without permission or a documented licence/legal basis.
- Attribute personal earnings claims to the creator and retain date, hours, city, platform and whether expenses are included.
- Do not imply a creator endorses a platform, product or claim unless they explicitly do.
- Do not expose private personal details or encourage unsafe riding for footage.
